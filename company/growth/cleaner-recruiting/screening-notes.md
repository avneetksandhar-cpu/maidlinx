# Screening notes — founder checklist (FL + GTA)

Quick fit screen for applicants from the **MaidLinx Cleaner Application**: https://maidlinx.com/earn.  
Operational checklist for founders/ops — **not** legal advice, **not** a background-check product claim, and **not** an employment decision framework.

**Markets:** SOUTH_FLORIDA = ACTIVE · TORONTO_GTA = ACTIVE (screen within the applicant’s market; do not blend).  
**Funnel per market:** **10+ applications → 5 screened → 3 strong → 2+ bookable** in the **same** initial area.  
**Outcomes:** Application → Screened → Strong → Bookable only after zones/services/availability/ops are complete.

**Explicit:** Leave customer booking OFF and Stripe LIVE OFF until that supply exists. Do **not** enable `booking_enabled` or activate cleaner `ba902d50` from recruiting alone. Do **not** invent cleaner data. Log source in `../applicant-source-tracker.md` when known.

---

## Promising / strong (advance)

| Area | Promising / strong signal |
|------|---------------------------|
| Experience | Prior residential cleaning (homes/condos); can describe a typical job |
| Supplies | Owns or can bring basic supplies / vacuum as needed |
| Transport | Reliable way to reach jobs in the target zone (car often needed in FL; GTA transit+reliability honest) |
| Zones | Lives/works near the **initial** focus area — FL: Miami-Dade / Broward / Palm Beach depth · GTA: Toronto / Brampton / Mississauga depth; willing to start narrow |
| Reliability | Communicates clearly; shows up on time; realistic schedule |
| Role clarity | Understands **independent contractor**, flexible bookings, **no guaranteed volume** |

Optional pluses: past client references, own insurance, bilingual if relevant to zone.

---

## Reject / deprioritize

| Area | Reject / park signal |
|------|----------------------|
| Experience | No cleaning experience and no plan to learn; unrelated work only |
| Supplies | Expects MaidLinx to outfit them as an employee |
| Transport | Cannot reach the initial zone reliably |
| Zones | Only available far outside launch area with no near-term focus |
| Reliability | Ghosts messages, hostile, or demands guaranteed hours/salary |
| Role clarity | Insists on employment / W-2 / guaranteed income; refuses IC framing |
| Integrity | Spam, fake profile, or pressure to skip application/verification |

When unsure: **park** rather than over-promising.

---

## Screen call / message (5–10 min)

1. Confirm they applied via https://maidlinx.com/earn.  
2. Confirm market + preferred first zone (FL or GTA depth — not “everywhere”).  
3. Ask how they heard about MaidLinx → log source.  
4. Experience + supplies + transport.  
5. Availability windows — note only; not a commitment of jobs.  
6. Restate: IC; no guaranteed income; approval ≠ immediate bookings.  
7. Next step: continue verification / approval if strong.

**Do not say:** “We’ll keep you busy,” “guaranteed X jobs/week,” “you’re hired as staff,” or that background screening is complete unless a real process has run.

---
