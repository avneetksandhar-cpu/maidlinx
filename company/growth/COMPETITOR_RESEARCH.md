# Competitor research (public positioning)

**Purpose:** Understand how customers shop cleaning in GTA + South Florida.  
**Rules:** Public positioning only. No copying copy, scraping private data, or trademark misuse.  
**Date:** 2026-08-11  

MaidLinx differentiation to protect: **address-first online book → server-priced estimate → deposit → ops assign → cleaner lifecycle**, live in **Toronto/GTA + South Florida**.

---

## Category map

| Archetype | Who customers compare | Typical promise | Weakness MaidLinx can exploit |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| National marketplaces | Handy-class, TaskRabbit-class | Fast book, many pros | Inconsistent quality; generic national SEO; weak dual-market brand |
| Local maid agencies | Independent GTA / South FL companies | Trusted local team, phone quote | Phone friction; slow quotes; weak self-serve UX |
| Franchise brands | Molly Maid–class, Merry Maids–class | Brand trust, recurring | Less on-demand; sales-led; heavier packages |
| Property-manager tools | STR turnover specialists | Turnover SLAs | Narrow vertical; not homeowner-first |
| Gig apps | Generalist task apps | Cheap / flexible | Trust & cleaning specialization weaker |

Exact competitor set should be refreshed with live SERP checks per market (human + GSC), not frozen forever.

---

## Positioning axes (how to win without lying)

1. **Self-serve speed** — book online with upfront estimate (deposit model already in product).  
2. **Two live metros** — rare honest dual CA/US footprint; do not dilute with fake cities.  
3. **Catalog breadth** — residential, deep, move, Airbnb/STR, office, post-construction, event, recurring.  
4. **Ops-backed assignment** — not pure anonymous gig chaos; admin assign + cleaner portal.  
5. **Transparent payment** — deposit + balance after complete (explain clearly; never invent guarantees).

## What not to copy
